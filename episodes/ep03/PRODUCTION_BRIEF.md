# EP03 Production Brief

**Title:** Two Factor Authentication, Without the Confusion

Narration-first. Aunt Truth / Aurora Reid is the storyteller and voice-over anchor, not the default visual subject. Kirk is a preferred viewer-surrogate only where an everyday adult helps the story.

Core visual arc: copied password becomes a physical key; the second lock refuses access; show know/have/are factors; show SMS as useful but exposed to carrier/SIM-swap risk; show authenticator-app OTPs as avoiding carrier delivery but not inherently phishing-resistant; show passkeys and FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys as stronger phishing-resistant options when correctly implemented; secure email first; close on an innocent-looking message before the click to bridge into EP04 phishing.

Approved narration replacement:

> Turning on any second factor  
> makes your account harder to steal.  
>  
> Scammers are fishing for easy.  
> You just stopped being easy.

Constraints: no absolute security promises; no reference chaining; canonical character refs are identity source of truth; no incidental text/signage from reference sheets; no decorative hacker dashboards; human approval before media generation and publishing.
