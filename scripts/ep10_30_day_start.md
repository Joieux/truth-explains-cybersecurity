title: Starting Cybersecurity in 30 Days
episode: 10
color: purple

script

Hey, family.
Ten episodes ago,
I told you cybersecurity wasn't just
for people with server racks
and three monitors.

Some of you believed me.
Some of you felt something.

This episode is for you.

---

Here's the lie that stops people:
"I have to be a genius first."

You don't.
You need a *map* —
and the discipline to walk it
fifteen minutes at a time.

Here's the thirty-day map.

---

*Week one: learn the words.*

Phishing. Malware. Two factor. Firewall.
You already know half of these —
you learned them *here.*
Week one, you make them yours.
When the words stop being foreign,
the field stops being scary.

*Week two: learn the streets.*

How the internet actually works.
IP addresses. DNS. HTTPS.
Not to memorize —
to *recognize.*
Same as knowing your own city:
you don't memorize the map,
you just stop getting lost.

*Week three: get your hands dirty.*

Beginner labs.
Capture-the-flag rooms made for new people.
This is where it changes —
because reading about locks
and *picking a practice lock*
are two different educations.

*Week four: build something and show it.*

A one-page guide.
A home security checklist.
A write-up of one lab you beat.
Post it.

Because here's the secret:
in this field,
*proof of learning*
beats claims of knowing.
Every time.

---

And family — one more thing.

This field isn't one job.
It's a whole color wheel.

Red teams who think like attackers.
Blue teams who defend.
Builders. Automators.
People who write the rules.

Somewhere on that wheel
is a seat shaped like you.
You don't have to know which one yet.
Week one is just fifteen minutes.

Fifteen minutes a day
beats three hours once a month.
Momentum beats motivation.
Start before you feel ready —
*nobody* feels ready.

---

Alright, family.
That's enough —
not just for today.
That's enough for season one.

Look how far you came.

You walked into episode one
just locking your front door.
You're leaving with the keys,
the deadbolt,
the vault,
the safety net —
and now, maybe,
a map to a whole career.

I told you from the start:
you've already got what it takes.
That was never the question.
The question was whether you'd start.

So: pick a track.
Fifteen minutes.
*Today.*

Stay with me.
Tell me what to explain next —
the comments are open
and I read them all.

Understanding first. Tools second.
Always.

I'll see you next season.
We've got this.
