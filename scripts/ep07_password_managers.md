title: Setting Up Your Password Manager, Step by Step
episode: 07
color: yellow

script

Hey, family.
Today is different.

Back in episode two,
I told you *why* you need a password manager.
Today, we *build.*

Laptop out.
Twenty minutes.
That's all this takes.

Because a password manager
is not extra.
It's grown folks internet.

---

*Step one: pick it and install it.*

Don't agonize.
Any of the major, well-reviewed managers
beats what you're doing now.
Pick one. Install it.
On the laptop AND the phone.

Done? Good.
That was the step
people stay stuck on for *years.*

---

*Step two: the master password.*

This is the one password
you'll still have to remember.
So we make it count.

Don't make it a word.
Make it a *sentence.*

A weird little sentence
only you would say.
Long beats clever.
Every time.

Say it out loud twice.
Type it twice.
It's yours now.

---

*Step three: your email goes in first.*

Why email?
You know this from episode three —
your email is the *master key.*
Every reset link flows through it.

So today:
change your email password
to a long, strange one
the manager creates.
Save it in the vault.
Guard the master key first —
the whole ring gets stronger.

---

*Step four: lock the vault itself.*

Turn on two factor
for the password manager.
Now your vault has
a lock *and* a deadbolt.

---

*Step five: turn on autofill.*

And here's the bonus
nobody tells you about.

Autofill is a fake-site detector.

If you land on a page
and your manager *refuses* to fill —
pause.
Because the manager knows
the real site's address exactly.

If it's saying
"hold on — why don't I recognize this page?"
listen to it.
That hesitation saves people.

---

That's today.
Email only.
Do not do everything in one sitting.

Bank this week.
Socials this weekend.
One door at a time.

We're building a system,
not rushing a chore.

---

Alright, family.
That's enough for today.

You walked in with passwords
held together by memory and hope.
You're walking out with a vault,
a deadbolt on the vault,
and a master key under guard.

That's not a small thing.

Stay with me.
We're building this together.
Understanding first. Tools second.

Next time: the DMs.
Where scammers feel most at home —
and the one rule about codes
that protects your whole account.

I'll see you then.
We've got this.
