# Truth Explains — Narration-First QA Checklist

Use before final export.

## Story

- [ ] Episode has a clear hook.
- [ ] The cybersecurity concept is grounded in an everyday situation.
- [ ] The explanation shows cause and effect.
- [ ] The viewer receives a practical action.
- [ ] The ending resolves the episode's central idea.

## Aunt Truth

- [ ] Aunt Truth functions primarily as narrator/storyteller.
- [ ] She is not automatically shown during her narration.
- [ ] Every on-screen Aunt Truth appearance has a reason.
- [ ] Direct-address moments are intentional.
- [ ] Her visual presence does not replace stronger scene storytelling.

## Maya

- [ ] Maya is used only when a viewer-surrogate is useful.
- [ ] Maya does not become a second host.
- [ ] Maya remains visually secondary to the Truth Explains brand.
- [ ] Her reactions and actions represent believable everyday behavior.

## Supporting characters / NPCs

- [ ] NPCs are used when the scenario logically requires other people.
- [ ] NPC dialogue advances the scene or cybersecurity lesson.
- [ ] NPCs do not become competing hosts.
- [ ] Maya is not unnecessarily used for every ordinary-person role.
- [ ] Recurring NPCs remain visually and narratively consistent.

## Visuals

- [ ] Each beat shows what the narration is actually discussing.
- [ ] Visuals demonstrate rather than decorate.
- [ ] Concrete subjects are preferred over talking-head footage.
- [ ] Metaphors clarify rather than confuse.
- [ ] Lighting follows the design system.
- [ ] Normally lit scenes are not unnecessarily dark or moody.
- [ ] Camera choices support the meaning of the beat.

## Animation

- [ ] Motion communicates something.
- [ ] Animation is not added merely for visual activity.
- [ ] Cause-and-effect sequences are readable.
- [ ] Important actions finish before the edit cuts away.

## Text

- [ ] On-screen text is necessary.
- [ ] Text does not duplicate entire narration passages.
- [ ] Text is readable at intended platform resolution.

## Audio

- [ ] Aunt Truth narration is clearly dominant.
- [ ] Music supports rather than competes.
- [ ] SFX clarify actions without becoming distracting.
- [ ] Transitions do not interrupt narration.

## Edit

- [ ] Cuts follow changes in meaning.
- [ ] Shot duration supports comprehension.
- [ ] Aunt Truth is not held on screen merely to fill time.
- [ ] Visual rhythm supports the narration.

## Final two-part test

Mute the episode:

- [ ] The visual story still makes sense.

Listen without watching:

- [ ] The explanation still makes sense.

Then watch normally:

- [ ] Visuals and narration strengthen each other.

If all three are true, the episode follows the Truth Explains production
standard.
