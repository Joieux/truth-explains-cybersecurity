# Truth Explains — Content Plan
**Channel:** Truth Explains (Aunt Truth)
**Format:** Fully animated — AI-generated video (Veo 3.1) + Remotion assembly
**Cadence:** Biweekly (every other week)
**Audience:** Absolute beginners + people pivoting into cybersecurity
**Launch target:** March 2026

---

## The Format

This is a fully animated channel. No live filming. The workflow:

1. **Script** — clip-by-clip with dialogue per scene
2. **Veo prompts** — one prompt per clip (~8s each, ~25 clips per episode)
3. **Generate** — Google Flow Veo 3.1 Fast (20 credits/clip — not Quality at 100)
4. **Stitch** — Remotion or Premiere; generate clips individually, don't chain Extends past 10
5. **Finish** — add intro/outro graphics, background music, captions

**Character:** Auntie Truth — animated Black woman in her 50s, warm brown skin, reddish-bronze locs, cat-eye glasses. Boondocks-inspired, cel-shaded, bold outlines. Cozy home studio with exposed brick, walnut desk, "T" monogram mug.

## The Strategy

Two types of people are watching Truth Explains:

- **The curious beginner** — heard "cybersecurity" and wants to understand what it actually is
- **The career changer** — works in healthcare, finance, education, something else, and is eyeing the pivot

Both groups need the same thing: someone who speaks plain English, doesn't assume anything, and makes the field feel accessible instead of overwhelming. That's Aunt Truth's whole lane.

**Three content pillars:**

| Pillar | What it covers | Episodes |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| **Foundations** | What cybersecurity is, how it works, why it matters | EP01, EP02, EP12 |
| **The Pivot** | Career paths, breaking in, what employers actually want | EP03, EP04–07 |
| **Hands-On** | Real builds — home lab, Pi-hole, VPN, SSH | EP08–EP11 |

Mixing these pillars keeps the channel useful for both audiences and builds toward the book naturally.

## Production Checklist (per episode)

**Script phase**
- [ ] Script finalized (clip-by-clip with dialogue)
- [ ] Hook written (first 30 seconds — what's the opening line?)
- [ ] Educational graphics / visual anchors planned (what shows on screen)

**Veo generation phase**
- [ ] Veo prompt written for each clip (~25 prompts per episode)
- [ ] Clips generated in Google Flow — Veo 3.1 Fast (20 credits/clip, NOT Quality at 100)
- [ ] Output per prompt set to 1 (not 2 — saves credits, avoids duplicates)
- [ ] Clips reviewed for character consistency and lip-sync
- [ ] Inconsistent clips re-generated

**Assembly phase**
- [ ] Clips exported from Google Flow
- [ ] Stitched in Remotion (or Premiere)
- [ ] Intro (≤2 seconds: channel name + tagline, fade in)
- [ ] Outro (fade to channel name, no loud animation)
- [ ] Background music added (low, warm, not distracting)
- [ ] Captions/subtitles added

**Publishing phase**
- [ ] Thumbnail created (warm charcoal bg, cream text, muted gold accent — no glitch effects)
- [ ] Title written (clear, searchable, plain English)
- [ ] Description written (first 2 lines matter most)
- [ ] Tags added
- [ ] Scheduled/published
