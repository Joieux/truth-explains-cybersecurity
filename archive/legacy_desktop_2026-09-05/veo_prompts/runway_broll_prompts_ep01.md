## ⚠️ NOTES BEFORE YOU START

**B-ROLL 06** asks for three separate vignette panels. Runway will struggle
to generate three panels in one clip. Instead, generate **three separate clips**
(B-ROLL 06A, 06B, 06C) and cut them together in Remotion. Prompts for each
are provided separately below.

**B-ROLL 10** (the four protection rings) is motion graphics — Runway may
render it inconsistently. If the result looks off, try generating it twice
and use the better take. A simpler alternative prompt is also provided.

