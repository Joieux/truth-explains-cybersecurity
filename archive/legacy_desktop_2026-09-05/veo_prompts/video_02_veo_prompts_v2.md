**Narration:** *"First: you need a password manager. Think of it like a locked keychain that works for you. You create one strong master password — the only one you have to remember — and then the manager makes unique, complicated passwords for every single site you use."*

> **[B-ROLL 03]** — Generate fresh

```

Each key is distinct. No two are alike. The camera slowly rotates around the open lock, showing the variety. Then it closes again with a confident snap. One master key. Unlimited unique ones inside.

**Narration:** *"So even if one site gets breached, your other accounts don't fall like dominoes."*

> **[B-ROLL 04]** — Generate fresh

> **[LIBRARY: AT-LIB-08]** Stop. Firm palm toward camera. Clear and calm. Not angry — just firm.

---

> **[B-ROLL 05]** — Generate fresh

```

A small automated script icon — depicted as a tiny robotic arm — zips across the screen, tries the next entry on a list: P-a-s-s-w-o-r-d-1-2-3-!. Match found. A green checkmark appears instantly. The robotic arm doesn't pause — it moves to the next row on its list without missing a beat. The whole thing takes about two seconds. It was never a challenge.
