## Google Flow - Extend Feature Workflow

### ⚠️ WARNING: Extend Has Limitations

**Tested in Video 01 - Results were problematic:**
- After 25+ sequential Extends, video had duplicate clips, out-of-order segments
- Music/audio was inconsistent across clips
- Transitions were not smooth
- Some clips missing from final playback

**RECOMMENDATION**: Generate clips individually and stitch in video editor for better control.

### Why Use Extend? (For Short Sequences Only)

The **Extend** feature can help maintain voice and visual consistency for **short sequences (5-10 clips max)**. For longer videos, generate clips separately and stitch in post.

The Extend feature:
- Continues from the **last frame** of your existing video
- Attempts to maintain the **same voice** throughout clips
- Preserves **visual consistency** of the character
- Works best for **short extensions**, not full videos

### Step-by-Step Extend Workflow

1. **Navigate to End of Timeline**
   - Scroll right on the timeline to reach the end of your current video
   - Look for the "+" button at the far right edge of the timeline

2. **Click the "+" Button**
   - A dropdown menu will appear with options:
     - "Jump to..."
     - "Extend..."

3. **Select "Extend..."**
   - This opens the Extend dialog box
   - The dialog shows "What happens next?" placeholder text
   - Settings should show: "Veo 3.1 - Quality" and "x2"

4. **Enter Your Prompt**
   - Click on the text input field
   - Type your full prompt including:
     - Character description (Auntie Truth details)
     - Action/gesture description
     - Dialogue in quotes

5. **Submit the Prompt**
   - Click the arrow button (→) on the right side of the dialog
   - Wait approximately 60 seconds for generation
   - The timeline duration will increase when complete (e.g., 2:27 → 2:34)

6. **Verify Completion**
   - Check the timeline duration has increased (~7 seconds per clip)
   - The dialog will close or switch to "Frames to Video" mode when done

7. **Repeat for Each Clip**
   - Scroll to end of timeline
   - Click "+" button
   - Select "Extend..."
   - Enter next prompt
   - Submit and wait

### Simplified Prompts for Extend

When using Extend, you can use **simplified prompts** since the character and setting are already established from the previous clip. The key elements to include:

```
[Shot type] of Auntie Truth [action/gesture]. She is an animated Black woman in her 50s with warm brown skin, reddish-bronze locs, and stylish cat-eye glasses. [Expression/additional action] and says: "[Dialogue]"
```
