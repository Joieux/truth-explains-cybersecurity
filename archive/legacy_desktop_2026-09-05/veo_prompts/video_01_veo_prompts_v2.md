### Settings Reminder
- Model: **Veo 3.1 Fast** (20 credits) — NOT Quality (100 credits)
- Outputs per prompt: **1** (not 2)
- Generate each clip **individually** — do not chain more than 8–10 Extends in one project
- Upload `aunt_truth_definitive.png` as character reference before generating any AT library clips

### Assembly Order in Remotion
1. Import ElevenLabs narration audio as the base track
2. Drop clips in sequence order against the waveform
3. Library clips: cut on her breath or natural pause in audio
4. B-roll: cut at the start of the line being illustrated, cut back to AT on the transition line
5. Intro: fade in channel name + tagline (≤2 seconds)
6. Outro: fade to channel name + tagline (no swell, no loud animation)
7. Captions: cream text, charcoal semi-transparent box, center-bottom
