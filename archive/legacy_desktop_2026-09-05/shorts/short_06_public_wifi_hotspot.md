# Auntie Tip #6: Public Wi-Fi is a Public Room

[Action: Coffee Sip]
Think of public Wi-Fi like a crowded park. You wouldn't open your bank account or read your private mail in the middle of a crowd, would you? 

[Visual: Focus Scene - Bus vs Car]
If you're out and about and need to do something private, use your **phone hotspot**. It’s like a private car ride home—way safer than the public bus.
