# Auntie Tip #7: Trust your Password Manager's "No"

[Action: Coffee Sip]
Listen, baby. If your password manager usually fills in your login but suddenly stops on a site that looks real? **Stop.** 

[Visual: Focus Scene - The "No"]
That is your manager's way of telling you the site is a fake. It doesn't recognize the address, and that one second of hesitation can save your whole account.
