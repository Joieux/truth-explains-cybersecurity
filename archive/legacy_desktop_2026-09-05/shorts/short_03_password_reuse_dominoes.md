# Auntie Tip #3: Reused Passwords are Dominoes

[Action: Coffee Sip]
Family, reusing passwords is like having one single key for your house, your car, and your phone. 

[Visual: Focus Scene - The Dominoes]
If one person copies that key, they’re in everything. That’s not what we want.

Use a password manager. One strong master password for you, unique keys for everywhere else. 

[Visual: Pull back to Auntie's Desk]
It’s all about layers, baby.
