# Auntie Tip #1: The Big Three of Cybersecurity

[Action: Coffee Sip]
Listen, baby. Cybersecurity isn't as complicated as they make it sound. It really just comes down to three things:

[Visual: Focus Scene - The Stool]
1. **Confidentiality**: Keep your private stuff private. Simple.
2. **Integrity**: Keep your info accurate. Don't let nobody change your receipts.
3. **Availability**: Make sure you can actually get to your stuff when you need it.

[Visual: Pull back to Auntie's Desk]
That is the whole game! Most of those fancy defenses are just layers protecting those three.
