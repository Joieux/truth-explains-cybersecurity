# Aunt Truth Shorts: Production Guide

This guide details how to assemble the "Auntie Tip" shorts using the refined Aunt Truth character and the Boondocks-inspired urban loft aesthetic.

## Animation Structure: "Auntie's Desk"
- **Setting**: Urban loft, warm lighting, solid walnut desk.
- **Tech Setup**: Silver Mac Studio, dual Studio Displays, iPad Pro with Apple Pencil, Magic Keyboard, and Magic Mouse (silver).
- **Audio**: Pro-grade silver microphone on a sleek boom arm.
- **Opening (Frame)**: Aunt Truth is at her computer, looking at the screen, then turning to camera.
- **Transition**: Use a "Zoom to Monitor" effect. The monitor screen should expand to fill the frame, becoming the "Focus Scene."
- **Idle Action**: **The Auntie Sip**. During pauses or while waiting for a "focus scene," she takes a slow, classy sip from her signature ceramic mug with a gold 'T'.
- **Focus Scene (The Concept)**: Conceptual animations (see [Storyboard Scenarios](file:///Users/truth/.gemini/antigravity/brain/f9eb6226-072d-4e16-ab0f-48f2a7da5921/animation_storyboard_scenarios.md)).
- **Closing (Frame)**: Zoom back out to the loft. Aunt Truth is relaxed, gesturing to the viewer.

## Visual Style
- **Character**: Always use the **Aunt Truth Final Concept** (Dreadlocks, stylish glasses, classy urban outfit).
- **Background**: A sunlit, modern urban loft (Brick walls, large windows, minimalist furniture).
- **Auntie's Desk**: A solid walnut desk featuring:
    - **Computer**: Silver Mac Studio hidden under the desk or off-center.
    - **Screens**: Dual Studio Displays on a clean, hidden mounting arm.
    - **Tablet**: Silver iPad Pro with an Apple Pencil resting on it.
    - **Peripheral**: Silver Magic Keyboard and Magic Mouse on a leather desk mat.
    - **Audio**: Shure SM7B-style silver microphone on a sleek boom arm.
    - **Coffee**: The signature designer ceramic mug with a gold 'T'.
- **Animation**: Minimal movement for the frame scenes. **Key Pose: The Coffee Sip**—used during pauses to emphasize her relaxed authority. Punchy, dynamic movement for the focus scenes. 

## Format (YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (Vertical)
- **Title Overlay**: "Auntie Tip #[number]" centered at the top.
- **Captions**: Large, readable sans-serif text in the center-bottom. Use **Soft Cream** (#F5F5F5) for text and **Warm Charcoal** (#2C2C2C) for a semi-transparent background box.

## Assembly Steps
1. **Audio**: Use the locked ElevenLabs "Awnt Truth" voice settings.
2. **Opening**: Animated Aunt Truth intro at her desk (approx. 3-5 seconds).
3. **Transition**: Zoom into monitor.
4. **Visuals**: Focus scene animations based on storyboard metadata.
5. **Motion**: Slow fades between points. Use subtle "camera" pans to keep it dynamic but calm.
6. **Closing**: Zoom out. Aunt Truth sign-off with a warm smile.

## Color Palette Reference
- **Primary Background**: #2C2C2C (Warm Charcoal)
- **Accent**: #D4AF37 (Muted Gold)
- **Text**: #F5F5F5 (Soft Cream)
